App Installation

📱 Install Cashlytics as a native app on your device for better experience

📱 Mobile Installation:

  • Chrome/Edge: Menu → "Add to Home screen"
  • Safari (iOS): Share → "Add to Home Screen"
  • Firefox: Menu → "Install"

💻 Desktop Installation:

  • Chrome/Edge: Address bar → Install icon
  • Or: Menu → "Install Cashlytics..."

☁️ Cloud Backup

Secure your data! Automatically backup your financial data to your private GitHub repository.
✅ Free, secure, and you control your data.

🔒 How it works:
• Click "Connect GitHub" and follow the simple setup
• We create a private cashlytics-backup repository
• Your data is automatically backed up every 24 hours
• Only you can access your backup - it's completely private
• Use "Import Data" to restore backup on new devices
• Works across all your devices when you're logged in
